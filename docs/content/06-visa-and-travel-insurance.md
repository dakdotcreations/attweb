## Get your visa right the first time

A visa refusal costs you the fee, your time and sometimes your travel plans. Most refusals happen for avoidable reasons: a missing bank statement, an unclear itinerary or a form filled in wrongly. Our visa team helps you avoid those mistakes.

We guide Ugandan and East African travellers through visa applications for the world's most popular destinations, from the first checklist to submitting your application.

## Visa types we assist with

- **Tourist and visit visas:** holidays, family visits and festive trips
- **Business visas:** conferences, trade fairs and meetings, including invitation letters
- **Student visas:** for study abroad applicants (Malaysia and more)
- **Medical visas:** including the India e-Medical visa and medical attendant visas
- **Transit visas:** for connecting flights through certain countries

## Popular destinations

UAE (Dubai), Turkey, Egypt, South Africa, India, Thailand, Malaysia, China, the Schengen area (Europe), the UK, the USA and Canada.

*Please note: the final decision on every visa rests with the embassy or immigration authority. We can't guarantee approval, but we make sure your application is complete, accurate and as strong as possible.*

## How it works

1. **Free consultation.** Tell us where and why you're travelling, and we'll advise on the right visa type.
2. **Document checklist.** You get a clear, destination-specific list.
3. **Review.** We check your documents for gaps and inconsistencies before submission.
4. **Application.** We complete forms, book appointments and prepare supporting letters and itineraries.
5. **Follow-up.** We track your application and keep you updated.

## Travel insurance

Many visas, including every Schengen visa, **require travel medical insurance**. Even where it isn't required, insurance protects you against medical emergencies, lost luggage and cancelled trips. We arrange trusted cover for single trips, multi-trip travellers, students and senior travellers, often on the same day.

## East Africa: travel with ease

As a Ugandan citizen you can travel to **Kenya, Tanzania (including Zanzibar) and Rwanda** without a visa, and often with just your national ID for land crossings. This makes the region perfect for quick holidays such as Mombasa, Zanzibar and Kigali.

## The documents embassies usually ask for

Every country is different, but most applications include:

1. **A passport** valid for at least **6 months beyond your return date**, with blank pages
2. **Recent passport photos** that meet the country's specifications
3. **Bank statements**, usually for the last 3–6 months, showing enough funds for the trip
4. **Proof of employment or business**, such as an employment letter, leave approval or business registration
5. **A travel itinerary**, including a flight reservation and hotel bookings
6. **Travel insurance**, which is mandatory for Schengen visas
7. **An invitation letter**, for business, conference or family visits
8. **A yellow fever certificate**, required by many countries for travellers from Uganda

## Why visas get refused, and how to avoid it

- **Unclear finances.** Sudden large deposits just before you apply raise questions. Keep your statements consistent.
- **Weak ties to Uganda.** Show your job, business, property, family or studies to prove you'll return.
- **Inconsistent information.** Your dates, hotel and purpose of travel must match on every document.
- **Applying too late.** Some visas take weeks. Apply **4–8 weeks** before travel, or longer for Europe, the UK and the USA.
- **Incomplete forms.** Missing signatures and blank fields are a surprisingly common reason for refusal.

## Do you need travel insurance?

For **Schengen visas, it's compulsory**: you need a minimum of €30,000 in medical cover, valid across all Schengen countries for your whole stay. For other destinations it may be optional, but we always recommend it. A single hospital visit abroad can cost far more than the premium.
