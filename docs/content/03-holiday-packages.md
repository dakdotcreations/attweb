### Content

Every year, as December approaches, the same question comes up in offices, WhatsApp groups and family gatherings across Kampala: **Mombasa or Zanzibar?**

Both are beautiful, both are close, and neither needs a visa for Ugandan passport holders. They offer quite different holidays, though. Here's how they compare.

## Getting there

**Mombasa** is easy to reach overland. Many group trips (including our Twende Mombasa) travel by road to Nairobi and take the **SGR train** to the coast. The journey becomes part of the adventure and keeps the cost down. Direct flights from Entebbe are also available if you're short on time.

**Zanzibar** is best reached by air, either via Nairobi, Dar es Salaam or Kilimanjaro, or on seasonal direct routes. It's quicker, but flights make up a bigger share of the price.

**Winner for budget travellers:** Mombasa
**Winner for less travel time:** Zanzibar (by air)

## Beaches

**Diani Beach**, south of Mombasa, regularly appears on lists of Africa's best beaches, with long, wide, powder-white sand and palm trees. The sea is calm inside the reef.

**Zanzibar's** north and east coasts (Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Michamvi) have postcard-blue water. On the east coast the tide goes out a long way, so if swimming at any hour matters to you, choose the north.

**Verdict:** It's a draw, and both are stunning.

## Things to do

**Mombasa and the south coast**
- Fort Jesus and the Old Town
- Dolphin watching and snorkelling at **Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park**
- A dhow trip to **Wasini Island**
- Haller Park and the Shimba Hills
- Nightlife along the North Coast (Nyali, Bamburi)

**Zanzibar**
- **Stone Town**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with its carved doors, markets and rooftop cafés
- Spice farm tours
- Swimming with turtles
- Prison Island's giant tortoises
- The famous Rock Restaurant
- Snorkelling at Mnemba Atoll

**Verdict:** Zanzibar has more history and culture, while Mombasa has more variety on the way there, including Nairobi and the SGR.

## Atmosphere

**Mombasa** over New Year is lively and full of East African holidaymakers, with music, beach parties and a very social energy. It's great for groups of friends.

**Zanzibar** feels more like an island escape. It's relaxed, romantic and slower-paced, which makes it a favourite for couples and honeymooners. That said, the beach bars at Nungwi and Kendwa can be lively too.

## Cost

As a rough guide, from Kampala:

- **Mombasa group trip by road and SGR:** around **UGX 1.4m–1.8m** per person for 5 days, including hotel and activities
- **Zanzibar trip with flights:** around **UGX 3m–3.5m** per person for 9 days, often including all meals

The longer Zanzibar trip and the flights explain most of the difference.

## Travel tips for both

- **Book early.** December hotels on the coast fill up by October, and early-bird prices end quickly.
- **Carry your national ID or passport.** You'll need it at the border, at airports and at the hotel.
- **Pack reef-safe sunscreen**, a hat and water shoes for rocky reefs.
- **Respect local customs.** Both coasts are largely Muslim, so cover up when walking in town.
