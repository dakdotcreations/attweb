### Content

Renting a vehicle is one of the best ways to explore Uganda at your own pace. Before you book, though, there are some important choices to make, starting with whether to drive yourself.

## Self-drive or driver-guide?

| | Self-drive | With driver-guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | Lower daily rate | Higher rate, though the driver's costs are included |
| **Navigation** | You plan routes and handle road conditions | The driver knows every route and shortcut |
| **Wildlife spotting** | You watch the road | The guide spots animals and explains behaviour |
| **Safety and stress** | You handle traffic, potholes and breakdowns | Professional handling of all of it |
| **Park knowledge** | Limited | Knows where the animals are and talks to rangers |
| **Best for** | Experienced African road travellers | First-time visitors, families and photographers |

**Our recommendation:** for most visitors, especially first-timers, a **driver-guide is well worth the extra cost**. Uganda's roads can be challenging, and a good guide turns a drive into a safari.

## Which vehicle do you need?

- **Saloon car:** fine for Kampala, Entebbe and tarmac roads to Jinja or Mbarara. Not suitable for park tracks.
- **SUV (RAV4, Prado):** good for upcountry tarmac and well-kept murram roads, and for small groups.
- **Safari Land Cruiser with pop-up roof:** the best choice for national parks. It has high clearance, 4x4, and a roof for game viewing and photography.
- **Safari van or minibus:** for larger groups, though less capable in deep mud.

**Rainy season (March–May, October–November):** choose a proper 4x4. Some park tracks and roads to Bwindi and Kidepo can become very muddy.

## What does car hire cost?

Prices depend on the vehicle, season and whether fuel is included. As a general guide:

- **Saloon or SUV with driver:** roughly **USD 80–100 per day**, excluding fuel
- **Safari Land Cruiser with driver-guide:** roughly **USD 150–250 per day**, depending on whether fuel is included

**Don't forget these extra costs:**

- **Fuel:** a Land Cruiser uses a lot, and long distances add up quickly.
- **Park vehicle entry fees**, charged per vehicle per day in national parks.
- **The driver's accommodation and meals**, which are usually included by reputable companies, but check.
- **Tips** for your driver-guide at the end of the trip.

These times are approximate. Allow extra time for roadworks, police checks, rain and photo stops.

## Road tips for Uganda

1. **Avoid driving at night** outside towns. Poor lighting, pedestrians, cyclists and livestock make it risky.
2. **Watch for speed bumps**, which are often unmarked near trading centres.
3. **Carry your documents**, including a driving permit, vehicle papers and your passport or ID, for police checks.
4. **Keep valuables out of sight** when the vehicle is parked.
5. **Follow park rules.** Stay on marked tracks, keep to speed limits and never leave the vehicle except at designated places.
6. **Fill up when you can.** Fuel stations are scarce near some parks.
