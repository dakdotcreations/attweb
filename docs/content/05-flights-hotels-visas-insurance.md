For most trips out of Uganda, the air ticket is the single biggest expense. The good news is that with a little flexibility and the right advice, you can often save a meaningful amount. Here's what our ticketing desk at Tec Plaza tells customers every day.

## 1. Book at the right time

There's no magic day to book, but there are clear patterns:

- **Regional flights** (Nairobi, Kigali, Dar es Salaam, Juba): book **2–4 weeks** ahead.
- **Long-haul flights** (Dubai, Europe, Asia, the USA): book **6–10 weeks** ahead.
- **Peak seasons** (December, Easter, July–August, Eid): book **3 months or more** ahead. These fares rise steadily as seats fill.

Last-minute bargains are rare from Entebbe. Waiting usually costs you more.

## 2. Be flexible with dates

Moving your departure by a day or two can change the price a lot. Midweek departures (Tuesday to Thursday) are often cheaper than Fridays and Sundays. When you request a quote, tick **"my dates are flexible"** and we'll check the days around them.

## 3. Consider the connecting hub

From Entebbe, most long-haul journeys connect through one of a handful of hubs:

| Hub | Airlines | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Dubai** | Emirates, flydubai | The Middle East, Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Doha** | Qatar Airways | Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Addis Ababa** | Ethiopian Airlines | Africa, Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Nairobi** | Kenya Airways | Africa, Europe and Asia |
| **Istanbul** | Turkish Airlines | Europe, Turkey and the USA |
| **Brussels / Amsterdam** | Brussels Airlines, KLM | Europe and the USA |
| **Abu Dhabi** | Etihad | The Middle East and Asia |

**Uganda Airlines** also flies direct to destinations including Dubai, Mumbai, Johannesburg, London and several regional cities. Ask us about current routes.

The cheapest route isn't always the obvious one. A connection via Addis Ababa might beat a route via Dubai, or the other way round. Unless you tell us you only want certain airlines, we compare them all for you.

## 4. Look beyond the ticket price

A cheap fare can become expensive once you add:

- **Checked baggage** (some fares include none)
- **Seat selection**
- **Change and cancellation fees**
- **Long layovers** that need a hotel or a transit visa

Before you book, always check what's included. We explain this clearly with every quote.

## 5. Check visa and transit rules early

Some routes require a **transit visa** even if you never leave the airport. This can apply to certain connections in Europe, for example. Get it wrong and you could be refused boarding. Our team checks transit rules for Ugandan passport holders before issuing your ticket.

## 6. Group and student fares

- **Groups of 10 or more** (schools, churches, delegations, companies) can often get group fares with flexible name changes.
- **Students** travelling to study abroad can sometimes get extra baggage allowance and flexible return dates.

## 7. Book through a real agent

Online booking sites are convenient until something goes wrong. When you book with Altura:

- You get **one contact** for changes, delays and cancellations.
- We handle **rebooking and refunds** with the airline for you.
- We can bundle your **visa, airport transfers, travel insurance and holiday package** into one plan.

Your passport is your ticket to the world, but most destinations still need a visa. Rules change regularly, so always confirm the latest requirements before you book. Below is a practical overview from our visa desk, based on the questions Ugandan travellers ask us most.

*Visa rules change frequently. Contact us for the current requirements for your trip.*

## East Africa: travel with ease

As a Ugandan citizen you can travel to **Kenya, Tanzania (including Zanzibar) and Rwanda** without a visa, and often with just your national ID for land crossings. This makes the region perfect for quick holidays such as Mombasa, Zanzibar and Kigali.

## Popular destinations that need a visa in advance

| Destination | Typical visa | How it's done |
|---|---|---|
| **UAE (Dubai)** | Visit visa (30 or 60 days) | Applied online through an agent or airline before travel |
| **Turkey** | Tourist or business visa | Embassy application with supporting documents |
| **Egypt** | Tourist visa | Embassy application before travel |
| **South Africa** | Visitor visa | Application through the visa facilitation centre |
| **India** | e-Tourist, e-Business or e-Medical visa | Online e-Visa |
| **Thailand** | Tourist visa | Online e-Visa |
| **Malaysia** | Student pass or visit visa | eVISA, or the student pass via the university |
| **Schengen area (Europe)** | Short-stay Schengen visa | Through the embassy or its visa centre, with biometrics |
| **United Kingdom** | Standard visitor visa | Online application plus biometrics |
| **USA** | B1/B2 visitor visa | Online application plus an embassy interview |

## The documents embassies usually ask for

Every country is different, but most applications include:

1. **A passport** valid for at least **6 months beyond your return date**, with blank pages
2. **Recent passport photos** that meet the country's specifications
3. **Bank statements**, usually for the last 3–6 months, showing enough funds for the trip
4. **Proof of employment or business**, such as an employment letter, leave approval or business registration
5. **A travel itinerary**, including a flight reservation and hotel bookings
6. **Travel insurance**, which is mandatory for Schengen visas
7. **An invitation letter**, for business, conference or family visits
8. **A yellow fever certificate**, required by many countries for travellers from Uganda

## Why visas get refused, and how to avoid it

- **Unclear finances.** Sudden large deposits just before you apply raise questions. Keep your statements consistent.
- **Weak ties to Uganda.** Show your job, business, property, family or studies to prove you'll return.
- **Inconsistent information.** Your dates, hotel and purpose of travel must match on every document.
- **Applying too late.** Some visas take weeks. Apply **4–8 weeks** before travel, or longer for Europe, the UK and the USA.
- **Incomplete forms.** Missing signatures and blank fields are a surprisingly common reason for refusal.

## Do you need travel insurance?

For **Schengen visas, it's compulsory**: you need a minimum of €30,000 in medical cover, valid across all Schengen countries for your whole stay. For other destinations it may be optional, but we always recommend it. A single hospital visit abroad can cost far more than the premium.
