### Content

For most trips out of Uganda, the air ticket is the single biggest expense. The good news is that with a little flexibility and the right advice, you can often save a meaningful amount. Here's what our ticketing desk at Tec Plaza tells customers every day.

## 1. Book at the right time

There's no magic day to book, but there are clear patterns:

- **Regional flights** (Nairobi, Kigali, Dar es Salaam, Juba): book **2–4 weeks** ahead.
- **Long-haul flights** (Dubai, Europe, Asia, the USA): book **6–10 weeks** ahead.
- **Peak seasons** (December, Easter, July–August, Eid): book **3 months or more** ahead. These fares rise steadily as seats fill.

Last-minute bargains are rare from Entebbe. Waiting usually costs you more.

## 2. Be flexible with dates

Moving your departure by a day or two can change the price a lot. Midweek departures (Tuesday to Thursday) are often cheaper than Fridays and Sundays. When you request a quote, tick **"my dates are flexible"** and we'll check the days around them.

## 3. Consider the connecting hub

From Entebbe, most long-haul journeys connect through one of a handful of hubs:

| Hub | Airlines | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Dubai** | Emirates, flydubai | The Middle East, Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Doha** | Qatar Airways | Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Addis Ababa** | Ethiopian Airlines | Africa, Asia, Europe and the USA |
| **Nairobi** | Kenya Airways | Africa, Europe and Asia |
| **Istanbul** | Turkish Airlines | Europe, Turkey and the USA |
| **Brussels / Amsterdam** | Brussels Airlines, KLM | Europe and the USA |
| **Abu Dhabi** | Etihad | The Middle East and Asia |

**Uganda Airlines** also flies direct to destinations including Dubai, Mumbai, Johannesburg, London and several regional cities. Ask us about current routes.

The cheapest route isn't always the obvious one. A connection via Addis Ababa might beat a route via Dubai, or the other way round. Unless you tell us you only want certain airlines, we compare them all for you.

## 4. Look beyond the ticket price

A cheap fare can become expensive once you add:

- **Checked baggage** (some fares include none)
- **Seat selection**
- **Change and cancellation fees**
- **Long layovers** that need a hotel or a transit visa

Before you book, always check what's included. We explain this clearly with every quote.

## 5. Check visa and transit rules early

Some routes require a **transit visa** even if you never leave the airport. This can apply to certain connections in Europe, for example. Get it wrong and you could be refused boarding. Our team checks transit rules for Ugandan passport holders before issuing your ticket.

## 6. Group and student fares

- **Groups of 10 or more** (schools, churches, delegations, companies) can often get group fares with flexible name changes.
- **Students** travelling to study abroad can sometimes get extra baggage allowance and flexible return dates.

## 7. Book through a real agent

Online booking sites are convenient until something goes wrong. When you book with Altura:

- You get **one contact** for changes, delays and cancellations.
- We handle **rebooking and refunds** with the airline for you.
- We can bundle your **visa, airport transfers, travel insurance and holiday package** into one plan.
