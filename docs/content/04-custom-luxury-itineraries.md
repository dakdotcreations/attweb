## Travel designed around you

Some trips shouldn't come off a shelf. A honeymoon, an anniversary, a family milestone or a long-awaited journey deserves to be planned around **your** pace, **your** tastes and **your** budget, not someone else's.

With a custom itinerary from Altura, you work directly with a travel consultant who listens first, then builds a journey around you. That includes the right hotels, the right guides and the details that make a trip feel effortless.

## How it works

1. **Tell us your idea.** Where you'd like to go (or just a feeling, such as "somewhere warm with history"), your dates, who's travelling and a rough budget.
2. **We design a proposal.** Within a few working days you receive a suggested route, hotel options and a clear price in USD or UGX.
3. **We refine it together.** We swap hotels, add experiences and adjust the pace until it's right.
4. **We handle everything.** Flights, visas and invitation letters, hotels, private transfers, guides and tickets.
5. **You travel with support.** Our Kampala team is a WhatsApp message away for your whole journey.

### Content

Many travellers assume a tailor-made trip is only for the very wealthy. In reality, a well-planned custom itinerary often costs **about the same as booking everything yourself**, and sometimes less, because a travel company has negotiated hotel rates, local partners and the experience to avoid expensive mistakes.

Here's what really happens behind the scenes when you ask us to plan a trip.

## Step 1: The conversation

Everything starts with questions. Our consultants want to know:

- **Who's travelling?** A couple, a family with young children, a group of friends or colleagues?
- **When, and for how long?** Fixed dates, or flexible?
- **What's the occasion?** A honeymoon, birthday, anniversary or simply a well-deserved break.
- **What do you enjoy?** History, beaches, food, shopping, wildlife or adventure.
- **How do you like to travel?** Fast and packed, or slow and relaxed? Boutique hotels or big resorts?
- **What's your budget?** A rough figure per person helps us recommend realistically.

There are no wrong answers. "We'd like somewhere warm in December, under USD 1,500 each" is a perfectly good start.

## Step 2: The proposal

Within a few working days you'll receive a written proposal with:

- A suggested **route** and the number of nights in each place
- **Hotel options**, often with a standard and an upgraded choice
- Included **tours, transfers and experiences**
- A clear **price per person** in USD or UGX
- What's **not included**, so there are no surprises later

## Step 3: Fine-tuning

This is where a custom trip really earns its name. You might swap a hotel, add a night by the beach, include a private dinner on a yacht or drop a tour you're not excited about. We adjust the plan until it feels right.

## Step 4: Booking and paperwork

Once you're happy, we secure everything:

- **Flights** at the best available fares
- **Hotels** and **ground arrangements**
- **Visa applications**, including invitation letters and supporting documents
- **Travel insurance** where required

You'll receive a final travel pack with your vouchers, contacts and practical information.

## Step 5: On the road

During your trip our team in Kampala is available on WhatsApp. If a flight is delayed, a transfer runs late or you'd like to add an excursion, you message one person who already knows your whole trip.

## What drives the price?

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Season** | December, Easter and July–August are the most expensive times almost everywhere. |
| **Hotel category** | Moving from 4★ to 5★ is often the biggest single change in cost. |
| **Private vs shared** | Private guides and vehicles cost more but give you full flexibility. |
| **Flights** | Booking 6–8 weeks ahead usually saves a lot. |
| **Group size** | Costs such as vehicles and guides are shared, so larger groups pay less each. |

## Five ways to get more for your budget

1. **Travel just outside peak season.** Early December or mid-January can be far cheaper than Christmas week.
2. **Mix hotel levels.** Splurge on one special stay and choose comfortable mid-range hotels elsewhere.
3. **Combine nearby destinations.** Turkey and Egypt, or a safari and Zanzibar, reduce extra flights.
4. **Travel as a group.** Four or more travellers bring the price per person down noticeably.
5. **Book early.** Hotels and flights give their best rates to early bookings.
