# Altura Tours & Travel
## Business Context

This document contains business domain knowledge that should guide architectural, UI, UX, data-modeling, and content decisions.

---

# Company Purpose

Altura Tours & Travel is a growing tourism and travel company.

The primary objective of the website is:

- Establish credibility
- Showcase services
- Generate inquiries
- Improve professional image
- Connect customers with Altura's team

The website is not intended to automate the company's entire sales process.

Customer communication remains primarily:

- WhatsApp
- Email

The website should support and strengthen those channels.

---

# Customer Communication

Primary communication channels:

1. WhatsApp
2. Email

These channels are checked regularly.

The website should make contacting Altura easy.

The user should never feel forced into a complicated booking process.

---

# Website Success Metrics

Success should be measured by:

- More inquiries
- More WhatsApp contacts
- More email inquiries
- Improved credibility
- Better presentation of services

NOT by:

- Account registrations
- Online payment volume
- Self-service bookings

---

---

# Tour Package Philosophy

Packages should contain:

- Overview
- Price
- Duration
- Highlights
- Inclusions
- Exclusions
- Gallery
- Inquiry CTA

---

# Tour Packages Should NOT Include

Do not create:

- Day 1
- Day 2
- Day 3
- Day-by-day itinerary builders

Reason:

- High maintenance
- Low business value
- Not aligned with the company's current operational needs

Keep package management simple and practical.
