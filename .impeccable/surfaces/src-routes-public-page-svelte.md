# Public site: homepage, cards, gallery

Scope: homepage (Persuade); photo cards shared by tours and services; gallery (Experience). The inner pages follow the same world. Every service, medical tourism included, uses the standard service page; there is no separate medical layout (user decision).
Audience: overseas gorilla/safari travellers judging trust from abroad; Ugandans planning holidays, visas and flights on phones; anxious medical clients. Action: start a WhatsApp chat or an inquiry. Proof: own photos, real team (About page), real dated testimonials, UTA membership, Kampala Road office. No awards, ratings or invented figures.
User decisions: homepage stays short; team lives on About; hero is immersive and full-bleed (no border); hero shows the published services, not a price, and plays through all of them on its own; the visual style follows the user's reference video (full-bleed destination slider, condensed caps, photo cards queued on the right, round prev/next, progress line, big slide number); white paper is limited to one side of a card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Uganda's journeys shown as full-bleed photography with the place's name set huge in condensed caps; cards are the photos themselves. Refuses boxed white cards, stacked badges, icon grids and price-led heroes.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green grounds (950/900 alternating) and full-bleed photos under dark scrims; Oswald condensed uppercase for display, Montserrat for reading; a short gold dash marks every title; pill outline buttons, round icon buttons, one gold progress line. Stamp heritage kept as one-sided details only: perforated left edge on hero queue cards, a perforated paper ticket stub on tour cards, postmarks for provenance. No shadows, no gradients on surfaces.

STORY: One Kampala team plans any trip. The hero plays through every service; the visitor sees journeys with real prices, real letters, then WhatsApps or asks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed service photo filling the screen; gold dash and the service name in huge condensed caps left, summary below, round WhatsApp button and pill "Explore {service}"; the next services queue right as portrait photo cards running off the edge; under them prev/next circles, pause, a gold progress line and the slide number. Autoplays; the first queued card becomes the background.

FORM: user-pinned direction from the reference video (replaces the rolled Engraved Stamps world, whose stamp details survive as one-sided accents); seed:ec75641493dc0d0c. Signature: the service slider handover (queue card → background, progress line, number).
